## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-grade agricultural planning environment. The tone balances rooted rural empathy with modern agronomic precision. It feels organic yet structured, avoiding both raw utilitarian agricultural software tropes and slick, sterile corporate fintech aesthetics.

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, breathable negative space anchored by soothing, natural paper-like tones.
- High legibility and unhurried visual pacing tailored for both quick-scan executive evaluations and field-level sunlight readability.
- Purposeful micro-interactions with smooth, organic transitions rather than abrupt technological snaps.
- Clean outline iconography with uniform stroke weights paired with contextual status hues that evoke agricultural lifecycles (soil, water, growth, harvest).

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural earth and vegetative cycles:

- **Primary (`#164A34` - Deep Forest Green):** Used for primary calls to action, high-level structural containers, and dominant navigation headers. Establishes institutional permanence, stability, and trust.
- **Secondary (`#3D8B5A` - Crop Green):** Represents active vegetative growth and positive indicators. Used for success states, secondary buttons, progress steps, and key trend indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#4C9BB8` - Soft Water Blue):** Allocated strictly for irrigation metrics, weather advisories, rainfall analytics, and soil moisture states.
- **Neutral Base (`#F8F6F0` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas color. Replaces harsh digital white with an organic, sun-bleached linen tone to diminish optical fatigue in high-ambient-light environments.
- **Surfaces & Tints:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.93)`) for sticky headers, and Sage Tints (`#EBF3ED`) for contextual callout modules.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - Harvest Amber (`#D9902F`): Seasonal warnings, pest alerts, and advisory flags.
  - Earthy Terracotta (`#C85A45`): Critical crop failure risk, urgent drought warnings, and destructive action triggers.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Dark Charcoal (`#1E2924`) for body and headings, maintaining high contrast against warm ivory; Muted Earth (`#596A61`) for supporting metadata.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Subtle Warm Earth (`#E2DDD5`) preserves form boundaries without visual noise.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides high x-height, open apertures, and wide geometric curves, maintaining legibility when scaled across complex data dashboards or localized Indic script overlays.

### Rules of Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for seasonal summaries, crop yield projections, and top-level KPI metrics. Ensure headlines remain uncrowded; never stack more than 3 lines of display copy.
- **Body Text:** Standard body size sits at `16px` (`body-md`) and `18px` (`body-lg`) to account for varying vision proficiencies in outdoor daylight settings.
- **Numbers and Metrics:** Ensure numeric stats rely on tabular figures (`tnum`) when aligning market commodity prices, dates, rainfall amounts, and seed ratios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Flow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `16px` outer margin, `16px` gutters. High-density actionable cards stack vertically with prominent touch surfaces.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column layout, `24px` outer margin, `20px` gutters. Two-column split for crop metrics and planting timelines.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a maximum container width of `1280px`, `48px` outer margin, and `24px` gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated as an active structural tool rather than void area. Data-dense tables are softened with `space-md` internal cell padding, while high-level advisory dashboards leverage `space-xl` separators between analytical cards to preserve mental clarity and deliberate pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Stratification and Warm Ambient Shadows** rather than stark high-altitude digital elevations.

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Canvas `#F8F6F0`. Houses background structural grids and global layout wrappers.
- **Card Tier (Elevation 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with an ultra-subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 74, 52, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(226, 221, 213, 0.6);` and a hairline border (`1px solid #E2DDD5`).
- **Interactive Hover & Elevated Focus (Elevation 2):** Applied to active cards, tooltips, and calendar pickers: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(22, 74, 52, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(22, 74, 52, 0.03);`.
- **Modals & Overlays (Elevation 3):** Frosted glass backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(30, 41, 36, 0.4);`) paired with pure white dialogs cast with `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(22, 74, 52, 0.14);`.
- **Subsurface & Recessed Troughs:** Sage Tint (`#EBF3ED`) without shadow for nested informational panels, read-only soil properties, and system-calculated recommendation modules.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, organic, and approachable geometry via generous corner radiuses that mirror plant-like contours.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Feature `rounded-lg` (16px) curvature for a tactile, friendly grip.
- **Panels & Cards:** Feature `rounded-xl` to `24px` radius, giving large surfaces an approachable, calm presence without feeling child-like.
- **Pills:** Full rounding (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) is reserved exclusively for operational state badges (e.g., "Optimal Moisture", "Harvest Ready", "Advisory").
- **Structural Integrity:** Nested containers maintain concentric curves: parent containers at `24px` radius contain child inner nodes at `12px` to `14px` radius to maintain visual harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Forest Green (`#164A34`) solid fill, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` border-radius, `48px` minimum touch target height. Subtle downward scale on active press (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Crop Green tint outline or solid crop green (`#3D8B5A`), white or forest green text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent surface, Dark Charcoal text (`#1E2924`), hover reveals `#EBF3ED` sage background.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or translucent white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.93)`), framed with a `1px` stroke of `#E2DDD5`. Corner radius sits between `20px` and `24px`.
- High-level decision cards include a distinct left indicator accent (4px solid `#3D8B5A` for normal, `#D9902F` for attention, `#4C9BB8` for irrigation needs).

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pills (`height: 28px-32px`, `padding: 0 12px`).
- Moisture Chip: `#4C9BB8` background at 12% opacity with solid `#4C9BB8` text.
- Seasonal Stage Chip: `#3D8B5A` background at 12% opacity with `#164A34` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Resting Stroke: `1.5px solid #E2DDD5`.
- Focus Stroke: `2px solid #164A34`, accompanied by an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(61, 139, 90, 0.15)`).
- Text inside inputs defaults to `#1E2924` at `16px` to prevent automatic zoom on mobile interfaces.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and rounded-square outlines (`20px x 20px`) with a `2px` stroke of `#596A61`.
- Checked state transitions cleanly to `#164A34` fill with an organic tick icon in pure white.

### Specialized Agricultural Components
- **Weather & Irrigation Glance Bar:** Horizontal segmented bar using tertiary blue (`#4C9BB8`) gradients alongside warm amber icons showing 7-day soil moisture trends.
- **Crop Lifecycle Timeline:** A horizontal step milestone bar utilizing organic seedling-to-harvest line iconography with connected nodal dots showing progress, risk checkpoints, and harvest windows.
